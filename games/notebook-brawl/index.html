    How it works

    Versus: knock out the other stickman, or knock them off the bottom of the page. Gang vs Robots: team up and survive as many robot waves as you can.

    Your special bar fills every 10 seconds. Each weapon has its own special.

    Online party: up to 5 players on different computers. Open the game file in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, then one person hosts and shares the 5-letter code. Everyone plays with either set of keys.

    Get hit 4 times in a row and you break free and can't be hit for a moment, so nobody gets stuck.

    Power-ups: heart heals fully, shield blocks damage for 6 seconds, attack up makes hits 50% stronger for 8 seconds. Esc pauses.
