Sock Gobbler — Cousin Arcade

How to play
  • You are Gobbles, a fuzzy sock monster. Gobble every lost sock on the laundry room floor to clear the level.
  • Dodge the Dust Bunnies: Fuzz chases you, Lint sneaks ahead, Clump wanders, and Static gets faster as the level goes on.
  • Grab a Dryer Sheet and the bunnies get fluffed. Bounce them back to the lint trap for 200, 400, 800, 1600!
  • Snack on the duck, coin and cookie when they pop up. Extra life at 10,000. The dryer vents on the sides wrap you across.
←↑↓→ or WASD to move, P pause, M mute. Swipe anywhere on the screen to turn, or use the D-pad. Tap ❚❚ to pause.
