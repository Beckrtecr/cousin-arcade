Picnic PanicDefend the cake from the bug parade!

How to play: bugs march along the dirt trail toward your cake. Every bug that reaches it eats a slice. Pick a defender from the bottom bar, then click or tap an empty grass spot to put it there. Click a defender to upgrade or sell it. Squashed bugs drop crumbs to spend. Survive all 20 waves (watch out for the Queen Ant!) to win.
Keys: 1–5 pick a defender, Space send the next wave, U upgrade, S sell, F speed, P pause, Esc cancel.
