Duck DashHop across the busy town to the pond and beyond!

🪙 0 coins saved
How to play: hop forward as far as you can. Dodge cars and trucks, ride the drifting logs and lily pads across rivers (don't fall in!), and stay off the tracks when the light flashes. Grab 🪙 coins to unlock new hoppers: a frog, a penguin, a bunny, a kitty, a piglet, a hedgehog and more. Don't dawdle, or the hungry hawk swoops in!
Keys: ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to hop, P pause, M sound. You can also click to hop forward. Touch: tap anywhere to hop forward, swipe left, right or down to hop that way.
